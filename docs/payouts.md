# Payouts (AuraLabs Creator Circle)

- Monthly pool: $2,000 USD, equal share across accepted pieces that month.
- Method: the payment details on file in the member portal (demo: "payment method on file").
- Timing: by the 15th of the month after acceptance.
- Holds: missing tax form, disputed ownership, or a rules violation pauses payout until the manager clears it.
- Agents must never promise a dollar amount for a specific person. Cite this doc and escalate disputes.
