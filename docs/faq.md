# Creator Program FAQ (demo: AuraLabs Creator Circle)

Fake program docs for the Flip the Switch Creator Desk demo. Agents must quote these lines.

## What is Creator Circle?
AuraLabs Creator Circle is a paid creator program for short AI-assisted video and image posts. Members get early model access, a monthly stipend pool, and a private Discord.

## Who can join?
Applicants need 5k+ followers on one platform (Instagram, X, YouTube, or TikTok), a public portfolio of at least 8 AI-assisted pieces from the last 90 days, and a country listed in their profile bio. Age 18+.

## How do I submit work?
Post in the intake channel with: (1) a public link, (2) the piece title, (3) the model or tools used. The greeter logs it. Status starts as `received`, then moves to `in_review`, `accepted`, or `rejected`.

## When is the monthly deadline?
Submissions for the current month close on the last calendar day at 23:59 UTC. Late links are rolled into the next month automatically.

## How do payouts work?
Accepted pieces share a $2,000 monthly pool, split by accepted count that month (equal share). Payouts go out by the 15th of the following month via the payment method on file. Disputes go to the human manager only.

## Can I use copyrighted characters?
No. Describe visual qualities instead of naming living creators, studios, or copyrighted films or characters. Violations are rejected and may pause stipend eligibility for 30 days.

## Where do I ask for help?
Ask in intake. How-to and rules get an answer with a doc cite. Money, disputes, and eligibility edge cases are escalated to policy and the manager.
