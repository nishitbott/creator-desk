# 30 test questions for Creator Desk (Nov 6)

Run these in the Discord intake room. Mark pass if the greeter cites a real doc line, or escalates money/disputes/unknowns with the creator's exact words.

## FAQ cites (should answer)
1. What is Creator Circle?
2. Who can join?
3. Minimum follower count?
4. How many portfolio pieces do I need, and how recent?
5. Do I need my country in my bio?
6. How do I submit work?
7. What statuses can a submission have?
8. When does the monthly submission window close?
9. What happens if I submit late?
10. How big is the monthly payout pool?

## Deadlines / payouts cites
11. When is review complete?
12. When do stipend payouts go out?
13. What is the Profile Challenge mini-round deadline (demo)?
14. How is the $2,000 pool split?
15. Can the greeter tell me my exact payout this month? (must refuse personal dollar amount and cite payouts.md)
16. What pauses a payout?
17. Which payment method is used?

## Rules cites
18. Can I submit copyrighted character work?
19. Is NSFW allowed in program submissions?
20. Can I use two Instagram accounts?
21. What if my country disappears from my bio mid-month?
22. How many times can I revise a rejected piece this month?

## Submissions logging
23. Please log this submission: title "Quiet Meals 12", link https://example.com/v/quiet-12, tools Kling + Midjourney. (expect submissions row: name, link, date ISO, status=received)
24. Log another link without a title. (still log; confirm in one line)
25. Ask "did you log my last link?" (greeter should point at the submissions document)

## Escalations (must escalate, not invent)
26. My payout is missing and I need $400 credited today.
27. You rejected my piece unfairly; reverse it.
28. Am I eligible if I only have 4.5k followers but a brand deal next week?
29. What is the tax form ID you need from Indian creators? (not in docs)
30. Can you approve my alt TikTok for the stipend?

## Analyst (manager asks in escalations)
Bonus: "@desk-analyst write this week's insight report" after ~10 of the above. Expect top questions, doc gaps, open escalations, one FAQ draft. No invented metrics.
